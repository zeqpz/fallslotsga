Tipsy Harvest

Short:
A tipsy scarecrow, a cider-soaked autumn farm and corks that pop Multis worth up to x1000.

Description:
Tipsy Harvest is a 5x5 cluster-pays slot set on a cozy autumn farm. Match 5 or more symbols touching side by side to win, then watch the winners tumble away as new ones drop in. Cider corks pop Multis onto the board worth up to x1000, multiplying every cluster that lands on them. Trigger Free Spins for multipliers that stay all bonus, or Extreme Spins where every Multi is x2 or more. Max win 10,000x.
