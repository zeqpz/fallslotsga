A wide landscape background painting for a slot machine game (no text, no logo, no characters, no people in the foreground). Scene: a Thanksgiving farm in autumn at golden hour. Foreground: a harvested farm field with rows of orange pumpkins of different sizes, a few hay bales, dried corn stalks tied in bundles, scattered fallen red/orange/yellow leaves on the ground, a rustic wooden split-rail fence. Middle ground: big fall trees with red, orange and gold leaves (maples and oaks), leaves drifting in the air, a red wooden barn on one side. Far distance: a small Pilgrim village of the 1620s on a gentle hill - simple timber-frame cottages with steep thatched roofs, a little wooden meeting house with a short steeple, wisps of chimney smoke, a palisade fence - small and soft in the distance under a warm orange-to-dusky-purple sunset sky with a few stylised clouds. Keep the CENTRE of the picture calm and lower-contrast (a 7x7 slot reel board will sit over the middle), with the most detail on the left and right thirds and along the bottom. Leave the lower-left foreground fairly open (an animated scarecrow on a pole will stand there).

Art style: Rick and Morty cartoon style - a modern adult-animated sitcom look: clean black outlines of even medium weight with a slightly wobbly hand-drawn feel, flat cel colours with ONE hard-edged darker shade tone per colour (no airbrush gradients, no painterly texture), slightly lopsided goofy proportions, simple shapes, crisp and readable. Faces (where there are any) have big round white eyes with tiny dot pupils, wobbly eyelids and a loose grimacing or drooling mouth.
Lighting: one warm late-afternoon key light from the upper left; the shade tone always sits on the lower-right side of every form.
Palette: Thanksgiving autumn - pumpkin orange #E8742A, burnt orange #C4521C, harvest gold #F2B33D, cranberry red #A3263A, maple red #D8432B, warm brown #7A4A2A, dark chocolate #3E2416, straw yellow #EAC55E, sage green #8A9A4E, cream #F6E7C8, dusky purple sky accents #6B4A7A.
