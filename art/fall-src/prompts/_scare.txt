Full-body character design of a goofy DRUNK scarecrow / straw man tied up on a wooden pole, front view, standing pose for a game character, isolated on a transparent background, the whole figure and the whole pole visible top to bottom with the base of the pole at the bottom. The scarecrow: a burlap-sack head with a lumpy shape, a big droopy brown patched witch-style farmer hat with a few straw tufts poking out, stitched X mouth seams but a loose crooked drunk grin, half-closed heavy-lidded bloodshot eyes (one bigger than the other), a big rosy red nose and flushed cheeks, a little drool; a faded plaid flannel shirt (red and mustard) with patches, rope belt, denim overalls with one strap fallen, straw bursting out of the cuffs, collar and ankles; his arms stretched out along the wooden crossbar and tied to it at the wrists with rope, straw hands flopping; his body slumped and leaning to one side as if tipsy; a brown clay moonshine jug marked "XXX" hanging from a rope on one hand; a black crow perched on top of the pole's crossbar on the far side. The pole is a weathered wooden post with a crossbar (T shape).

Art style: Rick and Morty cartoon style - a modern adult-animated sitcom look: clean black outlines of even medium weight with a slightly wobbly hand-drawn feel, flat cel colours with ONE hard-edged darker shade tone per colour (no airbrush gradients, no painterly texture), slightly lopsided goofy proportions, simple shapes, crisp and readable. Faces (where there are any) have big round white eyes with tiny dot pupils, wobbly eyelids and a loose grimacing or drooling mouth.
Lighting: one warm late-afternoon key light from the upper left; the shade tone always sits on the lower-right side of every form.
Palette: Thanksgiving autumn - pumpkin orange #E8742A, burnt orange #C4521C, harvest gold #F2B33D, cranberry red #A3263A, maple red #D8432B, warm brown #7A4A2A, dark chocolate #3E2416, straw yellow #EAC55E, sage green #8A9A4E, cream #F6E7C8, dusky purple sky accents #6B4A7A.
